Shingle Roofing Services

Industry-leading asphalt shingle roofing

At NS Roofer, we specialize in professional asphalt shingle installations that stand up to Nova Scotia's demanding coastal weather. As a GAF-certified contractor ranked in the top 5% across Canada, we deliver premium workmanship on every project — from straightforward re-roofs to complex multi-layer tear-offs and full replacements.

Our shingle roofing services come with some of the strongest warranties in the industry. Depending on the product line you choose, you receive a 40 to 50 year manufacturer warranty covering materials, plus our own 10-year transferable workmanship warranty that protects your investment even if you sell your home.

Unlike many contractors who shut down during colder months, our team installs asphalt shingles year-round. We follow all manufacturer-recommended cold-weather procedures, including hand-sealing and adjusted adhesive protocols, so your roof performs flawlessly no matter when it goes on.
